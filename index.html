01 · About Pestana

A holistic vision for residential & commercial spaces.

Established in 2017–18, Pestana Interiors describes itself as a Pune-based firm focused on creative, cost-effective design solutions and transforming houses into warm, inviting homes.

The portfolio describes a group of innovative minds taking creative challenges and providing a wide range of design ideas across Pune & PCMC. The stated approach is to create cost-effective design wonders that reflect the people living in the spaces, with a focus on evolving, inspiring design and excellence.
